### Interpretation

This cycle distinguishes two separate results, and they point in different
directions:

| | Result |
|---|---|
| **Research / discrimination result** | Atlas demonstrated meaningful discrimination before Stage 8 — narrowing ten names to two eligible finalists, correctly separating a real cautionary variant (MELI) from a fundable one, and correctly blocking an unresolved name (GLNG) rather than guessing. |
| **Allocation result** | The final dollar allocation remained exploratory/random. It does not confirm or extend the research result. |

**This is a valid Pilot 2 learning result.** It is not repaired here, and
must not be repaired later, with a score, a ranking, an optimizer, synthetic
rationale, or post-hoc justification. If a future cycle wants the allocation
