## PILOT 1 LEARNING

> **PROCESS COHERENCE: DEMONSTRATED**
>
> **DECISION EDGE: NOT YET DEMONSTRATED**

### Process coherence demonstrated because

- Discovery allocated attention without allocating capital.
- LOW was stopped by underwriting.
- Four names reached capital comparison.
- No score or optimizer forced a result.
- Cash was not overridden without demonstrated edge.
- Human Stage 8 remained intact.

### Decision edge not yet demonstrated because

- No equity earned deployment against cash under an explicit
  market-expectations / "what is priced in?" comparison.

These two findings are distinct and should not be collapsed. A coherent
process that has not yet produced demonstrated decision edge is the
expected state of a first pilot — it is not a failure, and it is also not
evidence that the method works. Per `../06_PILOT_HEALTH_SCORECARD.md`:
**technical success is not project success; decision improvement is project
success.**

This learning is human-authored. No automated learning, model training, or
algorithmic feedback loop is initiated, described, or implied here.

## 1. Experimental "What Is Priced In?" Stub

`../01_DISCOVERY_CONTRACT.md` records **"What is priced in?" remains an
OPEN METHODOLOGY QUESTION.** It stays open. The following is a proposed
minimum qualitative stub for a future cycle, not a resolution of the
question.

**Minimum qualitative questions:**

- What operating / growth / margin assumptions appear necessary to support
  today's valuation?
- How does current valuation compare with the company's own relevant
  history?
- What must the market already believe?
- What specifically does Atlas believe differently?

**Explicit constraints on this stub:**

- No consensus-estimate infrastructure required.
- No precise expected-return model.
- No master score.

The stub is qualitative and human-answered. It introduces no data provider,
no quantitative filter, and no scoring of any kind.

## 3. Pilot 2 Stage 7 Qualitative Grid

Frozen prospectively for a future cycle. Each row is answered in plain
language:

- Named edge vs cash / status quo
- Named edge vs beta
- Uncertainty type
- Portfolio factor overlap
- Liquidity / reversibility
- Relevant time horizon
- Falsification condition
- Funding source

**Constraints:**

- **No weights.**
- **No composite.**
- **No optimizer.**
- **No automatic winner.**

The grid organizes human judgment for legibility. It does not produce a
result, and no cell or combination of cells selects an alternative. This is
consistent with `../03_CAPITAL_COMPARISON_FRAMEWORK.md`, which requires a
preferred action, a next-best alternative, and a plain-language rationale —
produced by a human, never computed.

## 4. Funding-Source Distinction

Future Decision Records must distinguish among:

- **New external capital**
- **Existing Treasury / cash sleeve**
- **Proceeds from reducing / exiting a holding**

These are materially different capital questions and must not be conflated.
Pilot 1's Stage 7 comparison used FICTIONAL NEW EXTERNAL CAPITAL, and the
distinction was recorded explicitly on the face of that record.
