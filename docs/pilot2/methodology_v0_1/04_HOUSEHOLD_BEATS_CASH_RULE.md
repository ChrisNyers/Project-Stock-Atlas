# 04 — HOUSEHOLD "BEATS CASH" RULE

> **QUALITATIVE HOUSEHOLD RULE — NO FIXED NUMERICAL RETURN HURDLE**

---

## Core Principle

> The Treasury / Cash Sleeve preserves **optionality** while earning a
> point-in-time near-risk-free return.

Cash is not a residual or a failure state. It is a real position with a
real benefit — the ability to act later — and deploying it means giving
that benefit up. The rule below exists to make surrendering optionality a
deliberate act rather than a default.

---

## Leaving the Sleeve Requires More Than

- a higher earnings yield
- a higher FCF yield
- a higher AFFO yield
- a story
- a cheap multiple

None of these, alone or in combination, is sufficient. A yield metric is
valuation context; it is not evidence of edge, and it is never differenced
against the Treasury rate as a spread purporting to prove edge.

---

## An Equity Earns Consideration for Deployment Only When

1. The investment thesis is **understandable**.
2. Atlas identifies a **specific variant view**
   (`01_PRICED_IN_VARIANT_VIEW.md`).
3. The difference from market expectations appears **economically
   meaningful**.
4. **Primary evidence** supports the variant view.
5. **Material uncertainty does not block deployment**
   (`02_TYPED_MATERIAL_UNCERTAINTY.md`).
6. The investment is **preferable to relevant beta**.
7. **Portfolio fit / capacity is acceptable.**
8. The expected opportunity is **compelling enough to surrender Treasury
   optionality**.

"Earns consideration for deployment" is the correct reading. Meeting all
eight does not entitle a candidate to capital — it makes the candidate
eligible to be weighed. The Stage 8 human decision remains separate and
remains free to decline.

---

## Explicit Constraints

> **Do NOT create a fixed numerical return hurdle.**

> **Do NOT freeze a Treasury maturity or yield.**

**Treasury maturity remains selected point-in-time**, based on optionality
needs, liquidity requirements, expected idle period, prevailing yields, and
likely redeployment horizon. No maturity and no yield used in any single
record becomes a constant of the method.

Treasury is a **competing use of capital**, not a valuation benchmark and
not a threshold any equity must clear.

---

## Package Governance

- Pilot 2 tests **differentiation, not breadth**.
- Same or smaller written Pilot Universe. **Do not broaden discovery yet.**
- Do not automate methodology.
- No ranking engine. No master score. No optimizer. No estimate feed. No
  sentiment system. No second market-data provider. No live brokerage. No
  tax engine.
- **No forced equity deployment.**
