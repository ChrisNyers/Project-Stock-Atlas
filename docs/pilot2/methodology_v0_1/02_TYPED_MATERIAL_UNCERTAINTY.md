# 02 — TYPED MATERIAL UNCERTAINTY

> **NON-SCORED TAGS — NO WEIGHTS, NO COMPOSITE, NO RANKING**

---

## Purpose

Replace one generic MATERIAL UNCERTAINTY bucket with typed, non-scored
tags.

Pilot 1 carried four names into Stage 7 under a single undifferentiated
ATTRACTIVE WITH MATERIAL UNCERTAINTY conclusion, which made them
indistinguishable from one another. Typing the uncertainty is what allows
different survivors to receive different, defensible treatments.

---

## Permitted Categories

- valuation / expectations
- demand / cyclical
- competitive / structural
- rates / financing
- execution / capital allocation
- governance / management
- evidence / data

**Multiple tags may apply** to one security. Tags are descriptive labels,
never inputs to a calculation.

---

## Required for Each Material Uncertainty

- **Category:**
- **Description:**
- **Does it BLOCK an increment? YES / NO**
- **Does it CAP potential size? YES / NO**
- **What fact/evidence would resolve or materially reduce it?**
- **Revisit trigger:**

The **BLOCK vs CAP** distinction is the operative addition. It converts
uncertainty from a uniform disqualifier into something that can produce
different treatments: an uncertainty that blocks stops an increment
entirely; an uncertainty that only caps permits a smaller one.

---

## Template

```
TYPED MATERIAL UNCERTAINTY

Security:              [ticker — company name]
Date:                  [YYYY-MM-DD]
Recorded by:           [human name / identifier]

--- Uncertainty 1 ---
Category:              [one of the seven permitted categories]
Description:           [plain language]
Blocks an increment?   [YES / NO]
Caps potential size?   [YES / NO]
Resolving fact:        [what fact or evidence would resolve or
                        materially reduce it]
Revisit trigger:       [what event or change prompts a re-look]

--- Uncertainty 2 (repeat as needed) ---
...
```

---

## Constraints

- **No weights.**
- **No composite score.**
- **No ranking.**

Tags are not summed, averaged, counted, or converted into a severity level.
A security with three tags is not thereby worse than a security with one.
Nothing here produces an ordering among candidates.

**Do not retroactively re-tag Pilot 1.** The Pilot 1 underwriting
conclusions stand exactly as recorded in
`../../manual_pilot_v0_1/09_PILOT_1_DISCOVERY_BASKET.md`.
