# 01 — PRICED-IN / VARIANT VIEW

> **EXPERIMENTAL QUALITATIVE FRAMEWORK FOR PILOT 2**
>
> Manual. Human-authored. Not a valuation engine.

---

## Purpose

Provide a minimum human-readable way to ask what the current price appears
to assume, and where Stock Atlas differs.

This addresses the gap Pilot 1 identified as highest-priority:
**"What is priced in?" remains an OPEN METHODOLOGY QUESTION**
(`../../manual_pilot_v0_1/01_DISCOVERY_CONTRACT.md`). This framework does
not close that question. It gives a human a disciplined way to ask it.

---

## Explicitly Not Created

- No consensus-estimate infrastructure.
- No reverse-DCF engine.
- No precise expected-return model.
- No master score.
- No ranking formula.

---

## Required for Every Underwritten Survivor

### A. Current Price Context

- Current price / valuation context.
- Relevant valuation measure(s).
- Comparison with the company's own relevant history, where useful.

### B. What Appears Priced In?

A plain-English statement answering:

> **"What must the market broadly believe for today's price to make
> sense?"**

May consider, as relevant to the thesis:

- growth
- margins
- durability
- cyclicality
- competitive pressure
- capital allocation
- interest-rate sensitivity
- other thesis-specific economics

### C. Atlas Variant View

> **"What specifically does Atlas believe differently?"**

"Differently" means differently. A restatement of the consensus in more
favorable language is not a variant view.

### D. Why the Difference Matters

> **"Is the difference economically large enough to matter for the next
> dollar?"**

### E. Evidence

> **"What primary evidence supports Atlas's different view?"**

### F. Failure Condition

> **"What would show Atlas that the market was right and Atlas was
> wrong?"**

## Template

```
PRICED-IN / VARIANT VIEW

Security:              [ticker — company name]
Date:                  [YYYY-MM-DD]
Recorded by:           [human name / identifier]

A. CURRENT PRICE CONTEXT
   Current price / valuation context:
   Relevant valuation measure(s):
   Comparison with own relevant history:

B. WHAT APPEARS PRICED IN?
   [plain-English: what must the market broadly believe for today's
   price to make sense?]

C. ATLAS VARIANT VIEW
   [what specifically does Atlas believe differently?]

D. WHY THE DIFFERENCE MATTERS
   [is the difference economically large enough to matter for the next
   dollar?]

E. EVIDENCE
   [what primary evidence supports Atlas's different view?]

F. FAILURE CONDITION
   [what would show Atlas that the market was right and Atlas was wrong?]
```

---

## Status and Limits

**This is an EXPERIMENTAL qualitative framework for Pilot 2.**

- **It is not an intrinsic-value engine.**
- **It does not produce a numeric expected return.**
- **It does not prove mispricing by itself.**

Its purpose is to prevent **"good company + apparently cheap valuation"**
from automatically becoming an alpha thesis.

A completed Priced-In / Variant View is an input to human judgment. It
selects nothing, funds nothing, and ranks nothing. Sections C through F may
all be answerable and the candidate still not earn capital.
