# 07 — REVISIT TRIGGER

> **EVERY PARKED ATTRACTIVE CANDIDATE MUST STATE WHAT WOULD BRING IT
> BACK**

---

## The Requirement

Every parked ATTRACTIVE candidate must record:

- **Current status:**
- **Why not funded now:**
- **What fact / event / valuation change would cause Atlas to revisit:**
- **What would make the thesis weaker:**
- **What evidence should be checked next:**
- **Review timing or trigger:**

Both directions are required. A revisit trigger that lists only what would
improve the case is a watchlist for confirmation; recording what would
weaken the thesis is what makes it a genuine trigger.

---

## A Parked Name Is Not a Backlog

> **A parked attractive name is not a permanent backlog and is not owed
> future capital.**

Parking a name records a judgment made on a date under stated conditions.
It creates no obligation, no queue position, and no presumption in favor of
the name later. A candidate parked three times has not thereby earned
anything.

> **Revisit only when the stated trigger occurs or a new material fact
> appears.**

Absent a trigger or a new material fact, a parked name is not re-examined
merely because time has passed or because attention is available.

## Relationship to Other Records

A parked name's typed uncertainties
(`02_TYPED_MATERIAL_UNCERTAINTY.md`) each carry their own revisit trigger.
This record is the security-level view: why the whole candidate is parked
and what changes that.

### BLOCK vs CAP — What It Does and Does Not Govern

**BLOCK vs CAP affects the CURRENT capital decision.** It is a statement
about the increment being considered now, not a standing permission to
reconsider later.

- **BLOCK** means the uncertainty **prevents an increment** until the
  blocking condition is resolved or materially changes.
- **CAP** means the uncertainty **may permit an increment now, but limits
  appropriate initial size.**

**Considering a smaller INITIAL increment because uncertainty CAPS size
belongs to Stage 7 / Stage 8 sizing. It is not a revisit.** That judgment
is made once, on the current decision, by the human weighing the candidate
at that time.

**Once a candidate is PARKED, CAP status alone does not authorize repeated
reconsideration.** A CAP tag is not a standing invitation to re-look at a
name at ever-smaller sizes until it clears. Nothing about a capped
uncertainty makes a parked name eligible to return on its own.

### When a Parked Candidate May Be Revisited

A parked candidate may be revisited **only** when:

1. **its stated revisit trigger occurs**,
2. **a relevant uncertainty is resolved or materially reduced**, or
3. **a new material fact appears.**

Where a parked name's uncertainty is marked BLOCK, the resolving fact named
in `02_TYPED_MATERIAL_UNCERTAINTY.md` is usually the natural revisit
trigger to state here.

### Increasing Beyond a Cap

**If a position was funded at a capped size, increasing beyond that cap
later likewise requires a stated trigger, material uncertainty reduction or
resolution, or a new material fact.**

The cap is not a temporary inconvenience that lapses with time or comfort.
Adding to a capped position is a new capital decision and re-enters the
same gates as any other.
