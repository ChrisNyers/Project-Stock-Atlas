# 06 — EXTERNAL / OFF-BASKET CANDIDATE

> **AT LEAST ONE PILOT 2 CANDIDATE MUST COME FROM OUTSIDE THE FROZEN
> PILOT 1 BASKET**

---

## The Requirement

Pilot 2 must contain at least one candidate **not** drawn from the frozen
Pilot 1 33-stock basket (`../../manual_pilot_v0_1/09_PILOT_1_DISCOVERY_BASKET.md`).

This is not a broadening of Discovery. It is a test of whether the gates
hold for an idea that did not arrive through the controlled basket — the
case where enthusiasm, social proof, or a compelling story is most likely
to substitute for underwriting.

---

## Permitted Sources

The candidate may come from:

- Chris / Karen
- an external investor or contact
- an article or research piece
- Yahoo / Seeking Alpha / Reddit
- another legitimate source

The source is recorded, not judged. A good idea from a message board and a
good idea from a professional both enter the same way.

---

## Entry State

> **SURFACED — EXTERNAL INPUT**

**No shortcut.** The candidate enters at SURFACED like any other. It is not
pre-promoted, not pre-underwritten, and carries no conclusion on arrival.
SURFACED ≠ UNDERWRITTEN ≠ DECIDED applies in full
(`../../manual_pilot_v0_1/01_DISCOVERY_CONTRACT.md`).

## Same Gates

**Same underwriting and capital-comparison gates as all other candidates.**

An external candidate that reaches Stage 7 completes the same grid
(`03_STAGE7_COMPARISON_GRID.md`), carries the same Priced-In / Variant View
(`01_PRICED_IN_VARIANT_VIEW.md`), the same typed uncertainty
(`02_TYPED_MATERIAL_UNCERTAINTY.md`), and must clear the same "beats cash"
rule (`04_HOUSEHOLD_BEATS_CASH_RULE.md`).

Novelty is not edge. Where an idea came from is recorded for auditability
and is never itself a reason to fund it.

---

## Package Governance

- Pilot 2 tests **differentiation, not breadth**. Admitting one off-basket
  candidate is not broadening the universe; the written Pilot Universe
  stays the same size or smaller.
- **Do not broaden discovery yet.**
- Do not automate methodology.
- No ranking engine. No master score. No optimizer. No estimate feed. No
  sentiment system. No second market-data provider. No live brokerage. No
  tax engine.
- **No forced equity deployment.**
