## Primary Learnings

### 1. Eligibility and funding are different decisions

A Stage 7 treatment of ELIGIBLE FOR STAGE 8 — FUNDING UNDECIDED conveys
eligibility only. The funding decision is a separate human act, and a
Portfolio Action is separate again.

> **Security Decision ≠ Funding Decision ≠ Portfolio Action**

### 2. "What is priced in?" should remain a modest qualitative framework

> **Observable regime → Atlas variant → evidence → falsification.**

Atlas must not claim certain knowledge of the market's complete belief set.

### 3. Typed uncertainty is useful

BLOCK and CAP materially affected decisions and **remain non-scored**.

### 4. Portfolio context can defeat an otherwise attractive security

VICI's increment was blocked on portfolio fit / capacity without any
downgrade to security quality. Factor overlap is not issuer equivalence —
VNQ is factor overlap only and is not issuer-equivalent to VICI.

### 5. One controlled off-basket candidate adds discovery independence

ORCL provided independence from the frozen basket without requiring broad
automated discovery — and received no shortcut for being external.

### 6. Treasury remains productive waiting capital

**Funding one equity does not require deployment of all available
capital.** Residual capacity deliberately remained in Treasury / Cash.
Treasury is not a fixed hurdle.

### 7. Red Team earned its role

Red Team earned its role at the point analysis begins turning into capital
judgment.
