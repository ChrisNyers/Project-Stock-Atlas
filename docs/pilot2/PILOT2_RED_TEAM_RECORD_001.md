## Pass 1 — PASS WITH FIXES

### Major fixes

**1. EARNS INCREMENT removed.**

The initial treatment phrase **EARNS INCREMENT** was too strong, because it
converted a Stage 7 security-treatment conclusion into an implied funding
decision. It was replaced with:

> **ELIGIBLE FOR STAGE 8 — FUNDING UNDECIDED**

**2. Funding source separated from treatment.**

A candidate's treatment and its potential funding source are recorded as
distinct fields. Naming a possible source does not authorize its use.

**3. Priced-in claims softened to observable regime.**

The priced-in methodology was corrected from asserted knowledge of market
beliefs to:

> **OBSERVABLE REGIME → ATLAS VARIANT → EVIDENCE → FALSIFICATION**

Atlas must not claim certain knowledge of the market's complete belief set.

**4. Stage 8 kept genuinely open.**

Stage 7 language was corrected so that no Stage 7 output presumed,
implied, or pre-committed the human Stage 8 decision.

### Summary of what Pass 1 caught

- premature funding-authority language
- market-belief overstatement
- Stage 7 / Stage 8 boundary leakage
