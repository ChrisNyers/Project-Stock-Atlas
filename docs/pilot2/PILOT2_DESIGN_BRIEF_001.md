## Core Purpose — Unchanged

> Find alpha and beta opportunities, underwrite them rigorously, compare
> them with the existing book and alternatives, and allocate capital
> intelligently.

Pilot 2 does not redesign this purpose. It tests one specific capability
Pilot 1 left unproven.

---

## Pilot 2 Primary Success Test

> **Can Stock Atlas distinguish among several attractive-looking
> opportunities well enough to evaluate them against cash, beta, existing
> holdings, and each other — and then fund none, one, or more than one on
> purpose?**

The operative words are **on purpose**. The test is whether the treatments
differ for stated reasons, not whether capital moves.

### Pilot 2 is NOT

- a single-winner stock-picking contest
- a requirement to deploy capital
- a requirement for cash to lose

### PASS

Survivors receive **different, defensible treatments with written
reasons**. Examples of legitimate differing treatments:

- earns an increment
- attractive but blocked by uncertainty
- attractive but worse than another survivor for the next dollar
- attractive but blocked by portfolio fit / capacity
- multiple survivors may earn capital if overlap and capacity permit
- none may beat Treasury

### FAIL

Several names remain in one generic ATTRACTIVE WITH MATERIAL UNCERTAINTY
bucket and cash is the only honest action **because Atlas still cannot tell
them apart**.

Note the distinction carefully: cash winning is not failure. Cash winning
*by default, for want of discrimination* is failure. The Pilot 1 result was
the former.

## Each Underwritten Survivor Must Carry

### 1. Priced-In / Variant View

Minimum human-readable questions:

- What operating / growth / margin assumptions appear necessary to support
  today's valuation?
- How does current valuation compare with the company's own relevant
  history?
- What must the market already believe?
- What specifically does Atlas believe differently?
- Is that difference large enough and underwritable enough to surrender
  Treasury optionality?

**Excluded:** no consensus-estimate infrastructure required, no precise
expected-return model, no master score.

### 2. Typed Material Uncertainty

Record:

- category / categories
- does this **block** an increment?
- does this merely **cap potential size**?
- what fact would **resolve** it?

**Excluded:** no weighting, no composite score.

### 3. Increment vs Small Alternative Set

Each survivor is compared as a **whole proposed increment** against:

- Treasury / Cash
- one relevant beta
- current book / related holding
- at most one competing alpha
- DO NOTHING

**Funding source must be named.**

## Sophisticated Investor Rules, Not Systems

These are rules a human applies. None of them is a system, a model, or a
piece of software.

### Book as source of capital

Existing holdings may fund a better opportunity. Do not assume all capital
is new money.

### Overlap and size

If two or more candidates earn capital:

- state meaningful factor overlap
- state proposed sizing logic
- do not treat correlated positions as independent opportunities

### Household bar vs cash

Write a short, human rule-of-thumb for what constitutes enough evidence and
edge to surrender Treasury optionality.

> **Do NOT create a fixed numerical return hurdle.**

### Research-time budget

Cap concurrent full underwrites. Research hours are scarce capital and must
be allocated as deliberately as money.

### Off-basket / external idea

Pilot 2 must include **at least one security outside the frozen Pilot 1
33-stock basket**. It enters under the same gates and receives no shortcut.

### Revisit triggers

Every parked ATTRACTIVE name must state: **what would cause Atlas to
revisit the name?**

### Beta as an instrument

SPY, or the chosen beta, is a **deliberate investment expression**, not
merely a leftover comparator.

### Opportunity-set quality

If no candidate competes with cash, ask whether **the candidate universe
itself was weak**, rather than assuming the methodology failed.

### Anti-dry-powder check

The Pilot 2 health review must ask: **did Atlas ever fund equity or beta
for a written, defensible reason?**

Failure to deploy is allowed. Perpetual inability to act is a methodology
risk.

## Pilot 2 — DO NOT ADD

The following are explicitly out of scope for Pilot 2 and must not be
introduced:

- broader Discovery Universe
- automated discovery
- automated promotion
- second price vendor
- OpenFIGI / identity expansion
- estimate feed
- sentiment system
- factor model
- tax engine
- live broker
- optimizer
- master score
- opaque intelligence layer
- automatic learning

**Do not treat ACN, RMD, TROW, or VICI as a must-buy backlog.** They remain
underwritten and unranked, owed nothing. Carrying them forward is permitted;
carrying them forward as obligations is not.

> **Use the same or SMALLER written Pilot Universe.**
>
> **Prove differentiation before breadth.**

## Minimum Pre-Start Work

1. One-page Priced-In / Variant View template.
2. Typed Material Uncertainty template.
3. Fixed Stage 7 comparison grid + funding-source field.
4. Short household note defining what "beats cash" means.
5. Rule that existing holdings may fund an increment.
6. Slot for one off-basket / external candidate.
7. Revisit trigger on every parked ATTRACTIVE name.

**Then run one manual loop.**

> **Claude automates only after the manual loop is proven.**

This is the standing sequence: **Method → Decision → Learning →
Automation**, and **prove the method manually before automating the
method** (`../manual_pilot_v0_1/00_PILOT_OVERVIEW.md`).

## Pilot 2 Judgment

**Primary question:**

> Did Atlas help evaluate several real-looking opportunities against cash,
> beta, and the book and produce different, defensible treatments — fund
> none, one, or more than one — that can be reconstructed later?

**If yes:** decision edge is beginning to appear.

**If no:** do not broaden discovery. Improve the decision methodology
first.
