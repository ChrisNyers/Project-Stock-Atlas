## The Two-Bar Substitution Rule

A challenger must clear **both** bars to recycle incumbent capital:

1. **Would Atlas buy the challenger if the capital were in Treasury /
   Cash?**
2. **If yes, is the challenger sufficiently better to justify taking this
   specific unit away from the incumbent and changing portfolio role?**

Eligibility from idle cash and eligibility to displace an incumbent are
different findings. PH and AMGN reaching ELIGIBLE FOR STAGE 8 — FUNDING
UNDECIDED establishes candidacy only; it decides neither bar.

**Portfolio role is part of bar 2.** Displacing an incumbent changes what
the unit does in the portfolio, not merely which security it holds.

**Valuation alone cannot authorize recycling.** A cheaper challenger is not
thereby a better use of an occupied unit.

**KEEP COST ≠ DO NOTHING.** Retention is an affirmative allocation decision
for the tested unit; DO NOTHING is a separate governance object. Both were
live at this gate and neither was resolved here.
