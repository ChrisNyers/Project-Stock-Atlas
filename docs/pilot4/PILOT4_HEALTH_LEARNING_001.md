## Health Verdict — PROCEED

Pilot 4 demonstrated that **an opportunity may deserve consideration from
idle cash without deserving capital recycled from a strong incumbent.**

---

## Primary Learning

> # CASH INITIATION ≠ INCUMBENT SUBSTITUTION

Deciding that a candidate is worth buying with idle cash is a different
finding from deciding it is worth displacing something already held. The
two questions were previously collapsed. They are now separate.

---

## The Two-Bar Rule

A challenger must clear **both** bars to recycle incumbent capital:

**1. Would Atlas buy the challenger if the capital were in Treasury /
Cash?**

**2. If yes, is the challenger sufficiently better to justify taking this
specific unit away from the incumbent and changing portfolio role?**

Clearing bar 1 alone is not sufficient. A candidate that would be bought
with idle cash may still fail bar 2, and failing bar 2 is not a criticism
of the candidate — it is a statement about this unit and its current role.

---

## Preserved Rules

- **KEEP ≠ DO NOTHING.**
- **Holding is an affirmative present allocation decision for the tested
  unit.**
- **Ownership creates no automatic incumbency protection.** The incumbent
  is tested, not exempted.
- **Valuation gap alone cannot authorize recycling.** A cheaper challenger
  is not thereby a better use of an occupied unit.
- **Overlap raises the funding / substitution bar.** Overlap is a higher
  hurdle, never a safety premium.
- **Freed capital may legally go to alpha, SPY, Treasury / Cash, or remain
  with the incumbent.** No destination is presumed by the act of freeing
  capital.
- **No sizing inferred.**
- **No real capital.**

---

## Symmetry of the Two Bars

The two-bar rule cuts in both directions. It protects a strong incumbent
from being displaced by a merely-attractive challenger, and it equally
denies the incumbent any presumption of tenure — bar 2 is a real test the
incumbent must pass, not a formality it wins by default.

Pilot 4's result is the first case: COST held the unit on the merits, and
PH and AMGN remain eligible candidates whose eligibility was never in
question.
