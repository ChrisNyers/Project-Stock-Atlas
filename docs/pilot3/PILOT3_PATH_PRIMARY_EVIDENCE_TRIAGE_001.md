## CONTEXT

Is the guided deceleration timing, seasonality, structural competition,
product transition, sales execution, or something else? **The evidence
reviewed cannot fully distinguish among these** — but two things are clear:

1. It is **not simply optics or overreaction to a normal quarter.** The
   revenue-growth deceleration is guided by the company itself and verified
   against a primary prior-year comparable — a real, forward, management-set
   expectation reset, not a market misreading of a clean beat.
2. Net-new ARR fell from $59M (Q3 FY2026, primary) to $37M (Q2 FY2027,
   primary) — a real sequence-level decline in the pace of new bookings,
   consistent with, though not proof of, the guide-implied deceleration
   being a genuine trend rather than one quarter's guide conservatism.

No primary evidence was found attributing the deceleration to a specific,
reversible cause (e.g., a one-time large-deal timing shift, a sales
reorganization effect, or a named competitive loss). Its cause, and
therefore its durability, remains **unresolved** — but the magnitude of the
deceleration itself is not in question.

## TENTATIVE VARIANT — tested, not assumed

> The market may be extrapolating a near-term ARR/growth slowdown into

**This variant does not survive primary-evidence review.** For it to
survive, the evidence would need to show the guided slowdown is
near-term/mechanical rather than a genuine forward reset. Instead, the
guide-implied deceleration is **management's own forward-looking number**,
verified against a real prior-year comparable — it is not a market

slower growth. Cash generation and buybacks are real (§FACT), but nothing in
the evidence reviewed shows the market is *wrong* to weight the growth reset
heavily; if anything, the market's reaction is a direct, proportionate
response to a real, guided number.

## MARKET-COULD-BE-RIGHT — strong, evidence-based

- **The deceleration is guided, not inferred.** A ~13%→~7–8% YoY revenue
  growth-rate reset, confirmed against a primary prior-year comparable, is
  a genuine forward signal from the company itself, not an artifact of
  comparison-quarter difficulty or analyst-model error.
- **Net-new ARR is falling in absolute dollars** ($59M → $37M across the
  two most recent primary-sourced data points), consistent with the guide
  rather than contradicting it.
- **Management's own AI commentary undercuts near-term optimism**: agentic
  AI work is explicitly described as "still in a proof stage," not yet a
  demonstrated growth driver, even as management is simultaneously arguing
  AI increases the value of orchestration. The two claims are not
  necessarily inconsistent, but neither provides evidence of imminent
  reacceleration.
- **RPA-specific competitive risk from agentic AI is a real, live industry
  question** the 10-K itself frames as a risk area ("numerous risks
  associated with the evolving market for products with AI capabilities"),
  even though the 10-K does not go so far as to name agentic AI a
  structural threat.

**This case is strong enough that a reasonable investor could decline to
fund PATH** on the growth evidence alone, independent of valuation.

## FALSIFIER

the deceleration should be treated as the base case, not the exception.

materially different, and much weaker, setup for a distinctive Atlas edge
