## Treasury / Cash Sleeve Interpretation

**Purpose:** park idle capital while waiting for a sufficiently attractive
future opportunity to earn deployment.

**This is cash management, not a fixed Atlas hurdle rate.** The Treasury
sleeve occupies a slot in the Opportunity Set as a competing use of
capital. It is not a valuation benchmark, not a threshold any equity must
clear, and not a constant of the method.

### Maturity selection

Treasury maturity is selected **point-in-time**, based on:

- liquidity / optionality needs
- expected idle period
- prevailing Treasury yields
- likely redeployment horizon

### Point-in-time implementation reference for this record

**3-month U.S. Treasury bill / cash equivalent as of September 1, 2026,
approximately 3.9%.**

This reference is retained from the Stage 7 record for continuity of this
fictional pilot.

> **Do not freeze that maturity or yield as a permanent Atlas parameter.**

Neither the 3-month tenor nor the ~3.9% level is an Atlas constant. Both
apply to this record only and bind no future comparison. A future capital
question must re-select maturity against the four criteria above, at that
time, on that day's yields.
