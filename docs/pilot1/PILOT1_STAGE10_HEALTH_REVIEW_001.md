> **The unresolved issue is whether Atlas can identify when an opportunity
> genuinely deserves deployment.**

`../manual_pilot_v0_1/00_PILOT_OVERVIEW.md` establishes that **DO NOTHING
and HOLD CASH are valid outcomes**. The Stage 7 result is the framework
operating correctly, not the absence of a result.

The open question is the converse capability, and it is genuinely open: a
method that can only decline is not yet a method that can allocate.
Refusing deployment and earning deployment are two capabilities, and Pilot
1 demonstrated one of them.
